SaleHoo Review 2026

Best for Supplier Research & Vetting

Updated 2026 · Affiliate link disclosed

SaleHoo takes a different approach than most tools on this list — it's not an order automation platform, but a supplier research directory designed to help sellers find and vet legitimate wholesale and dropshipping suppliers before committing to them.

What SaleHoo Does Well

  • Vetted supplier directory — rather than an open marketplace, SaleHoo's directory is built around suppliers that have gone through a vetting process, reducing the risk of working with unreliable or fraudulent sourcing partners.
  • Market research tools — includes tools for researching product demand and competition before committing inventory or marketing budget to a niche.
  • Educational resources — SaleHoo provides guides and training content aimed at sellers who are newer to sourcing and want to understand supplier vetting themselves, not just rely on the directory.
  • One-time or subscription access — pricing structured as a straightforward access fee rather than a percentage of sales or per-order cost.

Who SaleHoo Is Built For

SaleHoo is most useful for sellers who want to do their own supplier vetting and research rather than relying entirely on a platform's automated supplier matching. It functions more as a research and directory tool than an operational automation platform like DSers or AutoDS.

Pricing

SaleHoo is a paid-access product rather than a free-tier platform — there's a cost to access the supplier directory and research tools, reflecting its position as a curated research resource rather than a free automation tool.

How SaleHoo Fits Your Strategy

If you're early in choosing a niche or supplier and want structured research tools rather than jumping straight into an automated ordering platform, SaleHoo's directory-and-research model fills a different gap than the automation-focused tools on this list. Verdict

SaleHoo is best suited to sellers who want to invest time in supplier research and vetting themselves, using a curated directory as the starting point rather than fully automated supplier matching.
