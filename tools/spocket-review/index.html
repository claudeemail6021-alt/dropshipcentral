Spocket Review 2026

Best for Beginners — Curated US/EU Dropshipping Supplier

Updated June 2026 · Affiliate link disclosed

Spocket is one of the most-recommended dropshipping suppliers for sellers who prioritize fast US and EU delivery. It connects Shopify, Wix, and WooCommerce stores to a curated network of independently-verified suppliers, most based in the US and EU.

What Spocket Does Well

  • 2–5 day US/EU shipping — significantly faster than the 2–4 week shipping times common with overseas suppliers.
  • Curated catalog — suppliers are vetted before being listed, which reduces the quality-control burden on store owners.
  • Branded invoicing — orders can ship with your store's branding rather than generic packaging.
  • Free plan to browse — you can explore the full catalog before committing to a paid plan.
  • 20–30% recurring affiliate commission — one of the highest recurring rates in the dropshipping tools category, which matters if you're also running content or referral programs.

Pricing

Spocket's paid plans start around $39/month. The free tier lets you browse the catalog, but importing products to your store requires a paid subscription.

Who Spocket Is Best For

Spocket suits sellers who prioritize product quality and fast delivery over the widest possible catalog. It's particularly strong for boutique, lifestyle, and home goods niches where a US/EU-based supplier meaningfully improves the customer experience.

Verdict

Spocket is our top pick for beginners who want curated, reliable US/EU suppliers without having to vet each one manually. The free browsing tier makes it low-risk to evaluate before committing.
